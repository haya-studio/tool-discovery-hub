WHY THIS EXISTS

Useful beats famous.

Tool Discovery Hub connects real problems with useful tools. We start with Anki and language learning, but the directory is open to other categories.

Room for independent builders

A tool does not need a big audience to be useful. Listings focus on purpose, features, and limitations rather than invented ratings or popularity.

Our editorial approach

Initial listings summarize official sources reviewed on November 7, 2026. They are not hands-on endorsements. Unknown pricing or sign-up requirements are labeled. Featured cards are editorial selections, with no paid placement.

The directory is free, with no ads, checkout, or affiliate tracking added by us.
