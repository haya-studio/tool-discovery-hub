Browsing and searching

This is a static directory. Search and filters run in your browser. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, or local storage. Search terms and filters can appear in the page URL and browser history; avoid entering sensitive information. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs such as IP addresses and requested URLs.

External sites and forms

Tool links lead to third-party websites, which have their own privacy policies. If submission forms are enabled, they open Google Forms. Information you choose to submit is processed by Google and the form owner, not stored by this static website. Do not submit confidential information.

Editorial submissions

Submissions are used to evaluate tool listings and understand unmet needs. A contribution does not appear automatically. Do not include someone else’s personal information.
