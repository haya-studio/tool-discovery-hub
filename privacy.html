Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Browsing and searching

This is a static directory. Search and filters run in your browser. We do not add advertising scripts, cookies, or local storage. Search terms and filters can appear in the page URL and browser history; avoid entering sensitive information. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs such as IP addresses and requested URLs.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand site traffic and improve performance. Your browser loads a measurement script from Cloudflare and sends page-view and performance information to Cloudflare, including page URLs, referring page URLs, and loading and rendering timings. URLs may contain search terms or filter values; do not enter sensitive information in searches or URLs.

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage for analytics, or fingerprint visitors to track them over time. Cloudflare receives your IP address as part of handling the network request; according to its documentation, the RUM service discards it at the nearest Cloudflare data center and does not store it in its core databases or logs. Data may be processed outside your country or region. See Cloudflare?s measurement and data processing documentation and Cloudflare?s privacy policy.

External sites and forms

Tool links lead to third-party websites, which have their own privacy policies. If submission forms are enabled, they open Google Forms. Information you choose to submit is processed by Google and the form owner, not stored by this static website. Do not submit confidential information.

Editorial submissions

Submissions are used to evaluate tool listings and understand unmet needs. A contribution does not appear automatically. Do not include someone else’s personal information.
